FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I need a research project or data already?

No. Mentorship students work on a pre-approved topic with pre-gathered data — NCDB or AACR GENIE, depending on the template. You don’t have to find a project first.

What template do I get free, vs. in mentorship?

Free by email: the NCDB abstract template, behind ~100 accepted abstracts. Mentorship: the AACR GENIE template, for working with AACR Project GENIE genomic data.

Can I split the cost and the work?

Yes — for the $249 founding Abstract in a Week tier, the cost and research work can be split between up to three students.

Is publication or authorship guaranteed?

No. What’s guaranteed is a finished draft if you do the steps and submit data on time — and your money back if you follow the steps and still can’t finish.

How does the 1-week timeline work?

You start with a pre-approved topic and the template, then work through the steps with MD email guidance. The week assumes you follow the steps and submit what’s asked for on time.

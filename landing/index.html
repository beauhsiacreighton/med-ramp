The problem

The traditional research path is broken.

If you’ve tried landing a clinical research project, you already know the routine:

  • Sending 40 cold emails to faculty physicians, only to receive silence.
  • Joining a wet lab, only to spend 18 months doing basic data entry, with no authorship to show.
  • Waiting nearly a year for institutional IRB approval before writing a single word.
  • Watching application deadlines approach with an empty research section on your AMCAS or ERAS CV.

Medical school admissions committees and residency program directors do not care how many hours you stood in a hallway waiting for an attending. They look for one thing: demonstrated scientific productivity and research skill.

Med-RAMP teaches you to publish. Productively.

You bring the focus and hard work. We provide clear, timely instructions on next steps and mentorship. Together we strive toward your research success.

Our guarantee to you and to science

We respect your time and financial commitment. If you complete the writing milestones, submit your data interpretation on time, and follow the structural protocol, yet are still unable to produce a submission-ready abstract draft, we will refund 100% of your registration fee.

Scientific peer review is strictly independent, so no ethical program can guarantee journal acceptance or authorship by third parties. What we guarantee is the work we control: your finished draft.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior research experience or coding knowledge?

No. Our protocols are built for undergraduates, pre-meds, and medical students with limited research experience. Through our learning resources and mentorship, you’ll gain the skills you need to start publishing independently.

Do I need to bring my own lab data or IRB approval?

No. Mentorship projects use de-identified, verified national databases such as NCDB and AACR Project GENIE, so you don’t spend months waiting on an institutional review before you start writing.

Can I split the cost with classmates?

Yes. For the Abstract Masterclass and Manuscript Masterclass, up to three students can team up on a single project, work through the milestones together, and split the enrollment fee.

How does the mentorship work if there are no daily live calls?

It works like an attending physician’s workflow: high-yield, milestone-based, and asynchronous. You follow the structured modules to build your draft, and Dr. Beau gives detailed, targeted written review and edits at the key milestones. You learn by doing, with expert oversight keeping you on track.

How quickly can I list this on my AMCAS / ERAS application?

Once your abstract draft is finalized and submitted to a peer-reviewed medical conference or journal, you can document the work on your CV and application under relevant categories such as “Works Submitted” or “Conference Abstracts,” and use it as talking points in interviews.

About Med-RAMP

Empowering the Next Generation of Physicians and Researchers

Looking for meaningful clinical research experience and mentorship?

We’ve been there. Navigating the path to medical school is incredibly difficult, and finding productive research is a major hurdle.

We created a solution. The Medical Research Assistance and Mentorship Program (Med-RAMP) is a fully virtual program designed to connect ambitious pre-medical students with experienced medical student mentors.

When you join us, you’ll contribute to vital research projects focusing on rare cancers—analyzing data, writing, and pushing the boundaries of medical knowledge. Our unique model allows you to gain experience from anywhere with an internet connection.

What You Will Gain with Med-RAMP:

  • Real Impact: Contribute to meaningful research in the field of rare cancers.
  • Authorship Opportunities: Earn your name on abstracts, poster presentations, and publications.
  • Direct Mentorship: Develop meaningful connections with medical students who have successfully navigated the application process and can offer invaluable guidance.
  • Proven Success: Join a community whose members have received numerous acceptances to medical school.

Our Mission

To empower aspiring physicians and researchers by providing accessible research opportunities and mentorship, guided by the core values of Character, Competence, and Care. We are dedicated to helping you develop as a candidate and a future professional.

Our Core Values

Character

We uphold the highest ethical standards, fostering integrity, honesty, and professionalism in all our interactions and research endeavors.

Competence

We are committed to excellence, ensuring every participant develops the skills and knowledge needed for impactful medical research.

Care

We cultivate a supportive and collaborative community, prioritizing mentorship and the well-being of every aspiring physician.

Our Research Focus

Med-RAMP specializes in rare cancer research, utilizing large databases to uncover demographic and genomic patterns that can inform treatment strategies and improve patient outcomes.

Rare Cancer Demographics

We analyze population-based cancer databases like the National Cancer Database (NCDB) to identify demographic patterns, incidence rates, and disparities in rare cancer types.

Genomic Analysis

Our research explores the molecular and genetic characteristics of rare malignancies, contributing to personalized medicine approaches.

Treatment Outcomes

We investigate prognostic factors and treatment responses in understudied cancer variants to guide clinical decision-making.

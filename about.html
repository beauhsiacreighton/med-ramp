About Med-RAMP

Empowering the Next Generation of Physician-Scientists

Our Mission

Med-RAMP was founded with a singular purpose: to bridge the gap between undergraduate pre-medical education and meaningful clinical research experience.

We believe that every motivated student deserves access to quality mentorship and the opportunity to contribute to advancing medical knowledge, regardless of their location or institutional resources.

Through our fully virtual program, we connect ambitious pre-med students with experienced medical student mentors who guide them through real research projects focusing on rare cancers—work that truly matters.

Our Research Focus

Med-RAMP specializes in rare cancer research, utilizing large databases to uncover demographic and genomic patterns that can inform treatment strategies and improve patient outcomes.

🧬 Rare Cancer Demographics

We analyze population-based cancer databases like the National Cancer Database (NCDB) to identify demographic patterns, incidence rates, and disparities in rare cancer types.

🔍 Genomic Analysis

Our research explores the molecular and genetic characteristics of rare malignancies, contributing to personalized medicine approaches.

📊 Treatment Outcomes

We investigate prognostic factors and treatment responses in understudied cancer variants to guide clinical decision-making.

🎯 Thyroid Cancer Variants

Special focus on rare papillary thyroid carcinoma variants, including columnar cell and other uncommon histologic subtypes.

🩺 Clinical Applications

Translating our research findings into actionable insights that can improve diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment strategies.

📈 Big Data Analytics

Leveraging statistical methods and large datasets to identify trends and patterns that would be invisible in smaller studies.

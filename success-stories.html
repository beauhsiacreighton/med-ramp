Success Stories

Real students, real achievements, real medical school acceptances

Our participants have gone on to attend top medical schools across the country. Here are just a few of their stories about how Med-RAMP helped transform their medical school applications.

👩‍⚕️

Sarah M.

Johns Hopkins School of Medicine
Class of 2028

"Med-RAMP completely transformed my medical school application. I came in with no research experience, and within a year I had earned authorship on multiple publications and conference abstracts. The mentorship was invaluable—my mentor helped me not just with research, but with navigating the entire application process. When I interviewed at Hopkins, being able to speak confidently about my research experience and understanding of rare cancers made all the difference. I genuinely believe Med-RAMP was the key factor that set my application apart."

📝 5 Authorships
🎤 3 Conference Presentations
🎓 3 Medical School Acceptances

Michael Chen

Stanford School of Medicine
Class of 2027

"As a non-traditional student, I needed to demonstrate my commitment to medicine and research. Med-RAMP gave me that opportunity. The program's flexibility allowed me to balance research with my full-time job, and the virtual format meant I could participate from anywhere. I worked on rare thyroid cancer epidemiology, and that research became a centerpiece of my personal statement. During interviews, I could speak passionately and knowledgeably about my research contributions. The mentorship I received extended beyond research—my mentor helped me navigate the unique challenges of being a non-traditional applicant."

📝 7 Authorships
🏆 1 Publication in Peer-Reviewed Journal
🎓 4 Medical School Acceptances

Harvard Medical School
Class of 2029

"Med-RAMP opened doors I didn't even know existed. Med-RAMP changed everything. Not only did I gain genuine research skills in cancer epidemiology, but I also learned how to think critically about data, formulate research questions, and communicate scientific findings. My mentor became a trusted advisor who helped me refine my interests and supported me through every step of the application cycle. The confidence I gained from knowing I had contributed to meaningful research was evident in my interviews."

Emily Thompson

University of Pennsylvania Perelman School of Medicine
Class of 2027

"Med-RAMP was exactly what I needed at exactly the right time. I was taking a gap year and wanted to strengthen my application with research experience, but most research positions required long-term, in-person commitments. Med-RAMP's virtual format was perfect. I could dedicate significant time to research while also working part-time. The quality of mentorship exceeded my expectations—my mentor treated me as a valued team member, not just a volunteer. The research I conducted on rare cancer genomics became a major talking point in my interviews, and I could tell it genuinely impressed the admissions committees. I'm convinced that Med-RAMP was instrumental in my acceptance to Penn."

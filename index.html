About The Program

Your Virtual Research Opportunity

This program was created with the pre-med in mind. As a fully virtual program, you can contribute to cutting-edge research on rare cancers from anywhere with an internet connection, fitting valuable experience into your schedule.

Mentorship from Medical Students

We've been there. Navigating the path to medical school is challenging. Our program provides meaningful connections with medical student mentors who offer guidance on research and firsthand experience of the application process.

Publish and Present Your Work

Your contributions are rewarded with opportunities for authorship on abstracts, poster presentations, and publications. Med-RAMP participants have already earned hundreds of authorships, significantly boosting their medical school applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Med-RAMP, and what is the focus of the research?

Med-RAMP (Medical Research Assistance and Mentorship Program) connects pre-med students with medical students for mentorship and research collaboration. The research primarily focuses on rare cancers, analyzing data from sources like the National Cancer Database (NCDB).

What qualifications are required to join?

No specific qualifications or prior experience are necessary. We are looking for motivated students who are eager to learn, work hard, and contribute to our research goals.

What is the time commitment?

The program is flexible. There are no minimum or maximum time commitments. Participants are rewarded with more responsibility and opportunities based on the quality and volume of their contributions.

How will this program contribute to my education?

You’ll gain practical experience in research, data analysis, and scientific writing. This hands-on experience in cancer research will provide you with valuable skills and knowledge applicable to your future medical education.

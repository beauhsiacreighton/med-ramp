Dizziness Intake

A pre-visit history tool for your ENT clinic

Let's prepare for your visit

This short guided questionnaire collects a structured history of your dizziness so your ENT physician can review it before your appointment. It takes most patients about 5–8 minutes.

  • One simple question at a time
  • You can go back and change any answer
  • Your answers are organized into a summary for your doctor
Please note: This tool does not diagnose any condition. It only organizes your history so your doctor can evaluate you more efficiently. If you are experiencing a medical emergency — such as sudden severe headache, chest pain, difficulty speaking, weakness, or fainting — please seek emergency care immediately.
